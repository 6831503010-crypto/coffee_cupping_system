FIRST-TIME USER GUIDE

How To Use Our Coffee Cupping Form

Form 1: Aroma Test

Step 1: Score

Overview

The Aroma section evaluates coffee scent and give score based on its intensity and overall quality. This evaluation provides the vital first baseline for determining whether the coffee achieves specialty-grade status.

  1. Dry Aroma
    • Rates the scent intensity of the dry, freshly ground coffee on a 1 to 5 scale.
  2. Break Aroma
    • Rates the scent intensity of the wet coffee infusion on a 1 to 5 scale.
  3. Colour Intensity
    • Logs the visual roast level of the sample as Low, Medium, or High.
  4. Fragrance/Aroma Quality
    • Selects the final overall quality score using the official SCA 6 to 10 scale.

Step 2: Aroma Qualities


Overview

Select all applicable quality descriptors for the sample across the categories. This helps identify specific flavor characteristics and flavor profiles.

  1. Category Selection
    • Choose descriptors from available aroma families such as Floral, Fruity, and Roasted.
  2. Specific Attributes
    • Select sub-attributes to refine the flavor profile classification.
  3. Other Tasting Notes
    • Add custom descriptors not listed in the standard choices if necessary.

Step 3: Confirm Aroma Stage

Overview

Once all evaluations within the Aroma stage are completed and verified, you can confirm and lock the stage.

**NOTE: THIS ACTION IS IRREVERSIBLE, SO ENSURE ALL DATA IS ACCURATE BEFORE PROCEEDING.**
