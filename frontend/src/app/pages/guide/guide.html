Coffee Cupping Evaluation User Guide

Detailed overview of the SCA-based digital cupping form and session workflows.

1. SCA-Based Cupping Form

The digital cupping form follows Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) evaluation standards for structured scoring:

  • Category Selection: Evaluate key coffee attributes including Aroma, Flavor, Aftertaste, Acidity, Body, Balance, Clean Cup, Sweetness, and Overall.
  • Optional Notes: Enter specific flavor profiles and sensory observations in the notes field.
  • Check All: Use the "Check All" feature to quickly toggle all attribute evaluation checkboxes.
  • Submission: Submit scores per sample to calculate section and total scores dynamically.

2. Blind Mode

Enabling Blind Mode hides sample names, origins, and producer details during evaluation to ensure unbiased sensory scoring.

3. Organizer Finalization

Session organizers have authority to lock evaluations once all cuppers finish. Finalizing a session freezes score entries and generates official summary statistics.

4. Average Session Total & Result Visibility

The system calculates the Average Session Total by aggregating all submitted cupper scores. Final score results and breakdown graphs become visible across all session participants after organizer finalization.

5. Qualification Badges

Evaluated coffees receive qualification badges based on their calculated average score:

  • Specialty (80+ Points): Meets official SCA specialty coffee standard.
  • Commercial (<80 Points): Standard commercial grade classification.
