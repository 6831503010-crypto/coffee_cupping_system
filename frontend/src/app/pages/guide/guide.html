ABOUT COFFEE CUPPING SYSTEM

Discover the True Quality of Coffee

Our Coffee Cupping System is a web-based platform designed to simplify and improve the coffee cupping process. Coffee cupping is a method of evaluating and comparing coffee beans based on their aroma, flavor, and overall quality. Traditionally, this process relies on paper forms and manual calculations, which can lead to recording errors, misplaced information, and unnecessary effort.

Our system brings the coffee cupping process into a digital environment, making evaluations more organized, efficient, and reliable.

Our Features

Digital Cupping Forms

Record coffee evaluations digitally without relying on paper forms.

Automatic Score Calculation

Calculate cupping scores automatically to reduce manual calculation errors.

Structured Evaluation Process

Follow organized evaluation stages to maintain consistency throughout the cupping session.

Cupping Event Management

Create and manage cupping events for organized coffee evaluations and comparisons.

Centralized Records

Keep evaluation results in one place for easier access, review, and comparison.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make coffee evaluation simpler, more accessible, and more efficient through technology. By digitizing the cupping process, we aim to help coffee farmers, processors, roasters, baristas, and coffee enthusiasts focus on what matters most: understanding and appreciating the quality of coffee.

Every cup tells a story. Our system helps you record, evaluate, and discover it.
