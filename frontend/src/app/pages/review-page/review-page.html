FIRST-TIME USER GUIDE

Review Your Coffee Cupping Results

  1. Scores Summary
    • Displays individual and categorical sensory score cards (such as Aroma quality, Flavor, Balance, and Uniformity) for the evaluated coffee sample.
  2. Defects
    • Summarizes any noted defects or confirms none were found during the evaluation.
  3. Estimated Total
    • Calculates and displays the final aggregated total score out of 100 points for the sample.
  4. Descriptors & Details
    • Lists selected aroma and flavor descriptors alongside expandable toggles to view detailed intensity and cup breakdowns.
