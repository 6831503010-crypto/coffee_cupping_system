Form 2: Flavor Test

Step 1: Flavor Evaluation

Overview

The Flavor section evaluates the taste characteristics of the coffee sample and its overall flavor quality. The evaluation consists of six criteria, each scored using the official SCA 6 to 10 scale:

  1. Flavor
    • Rates the combined intensity and quality of the taste and aromatic sensations of the coffee.
  2. Aftertaste
    • Rates the length and pleasantness of the flavor and aroma sensations remaining after the coffee is swallowed.
  3. Acidity
    • Rates the brightness, crispness, and quality of the coffee's acid attributes.
  4. Body
    • Rates the tactile feeling, mouthfeel, and physical weight of the liquid coffee.
  5. Balance
    • Rates how well the flavor, aftertaste, acidity, and body harmoniously complement each other.
  6. Overall
    • Selects the final integrated quality score and personal holistic assessment of the sample.

Step 2: Flavor Qualities

Overview

Select the applicable flavor descriptors to identify the specific characteristics and flavor profile of the coffee sample.

  1. Acidity Intensity
    • Rates the perceived intensity level of the sample's acidity on a 1 to 5 scale.
  2. Body Level
    • Selects the physical weight and mouthfeel thickness level of the liquid sample on a 1 to 5 scale.
  3. Uniformity — Cup 1 to Cup 5
    • Evaluates the consistency of flavor across all 5 served cups, checking each cup to ensure they taste identical.
  4. Clean Cup — Cup 1 to Cup 5
    • Evaluates the lack of interference or negative taints from ingestion to aftertaste across all 5 cups, checking each cup that is free of defects.

Step 3: Confirm Flavor Stage

Overview

This stage checks the defects and sweetness of the samples across all 5 cups, ensuring the data is accurate before proceeding to the final flavor attributes section.

  1. Defects
    • Identifies and logs negative flavors that spoil the cup quality, classifying them by intensity as either a Taint (2 points deducted per cup) or a Fault (4 points deducted per cup).
  2. Sweetness — Cup 1 to Cup 5
    • Evaluates the presence of sweet flavor elements resulting from fully ripe coffee cherries, checking each cup that displays appropriate sweetness (Score: 0/10).

Step 4: Flavor Attributes

Overview

Select all primary flavor groups and nested aromatic descriptors that apply to the evaluated sample to map out its detailed sensory profile.

  1. Flavor Attributes Checklist
    • Selects specific flavor qualities across the official categories including Floral, Fruity , Roasted , Sour/Fermented, Nutty/Cocoa, Green/Vegetative, Spice, Sweet , Brown Sugar, and Other .
  2. Other Tasting Notes
    • Add custom descriptors not listed in the standard choices if necessary.
